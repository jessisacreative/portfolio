Jameel Prize: Moving Images is the seventh edition of the V&A's triennial award for contemporary art and design inspired by Islamic culture, history, society and ideas.

I joined the campaign with around eight weeks remaining, coordinating a fast-turnaround push across design, press, print, distribution, OOH and paid social while working with stakeholders across Bradford Museums, V&A, Art Jameel and Bradford 2025.

The activity combined broad cultural audiences with more targeted local outreach, including audiences across Bradford's Muslim communities and people interested in contemporary art.
