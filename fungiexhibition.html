The Great Leeds Fungi Exhibition brought together artists working across illustration, ceramics, textiles, zines, painting, print and photography.

I exhibited fungi-focused photography exploring decay, transformation and natural systems, while also documenting the exhibition as event photographer across the weekend.

The project sits slightly outside my commercial portfolio but connects directly to my wider photography practice and the visual approach I bring into cultural campaign work.
