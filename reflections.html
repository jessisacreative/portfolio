Client
Reflections Film & Media
Sector
Early years, healthcare & charity film
Role
Freelance Social Media Manager
Years
2022–2023

I was brought in to strengthen the social presence of a small production agency whose new business had historically relied heavily on word of mouth.

I developed social strategy and creative foundations, created branded templates, publishing plans and copy, then worked with the team to turn behind-the-scenes footage, bloopers, project stories and industry knowledge into regular content.

The work also extended into event design and capture, including promotional flyers, banners, exhibition graphics, photography and video at industry events.

December 2022 campaign result

+359%increase in reach during the “12 Days of Christmas” campaign
+238%increase in engagements during December 2022
+15.5%growth in Instagram followers during the month
