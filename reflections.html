Reflections
Film & Media

Freelance Social Media Manager, 2022–2023

Client
Reflections Film & Media
Sector
Early Years, Healthcare & Charity Film
Role
Freelance Social Media Manager
Year
2022–2023

I was brought in by Reflections Film & Media to strengthen the social presence of a small production agency whose new business had historically relied heavily on word of mouth.

I started by developing a social content strategy and creative foundations for the brand: what to talk about, how the channels should feel, what formats were realistic to produce regularly, and how content could showcase both the quality of the company’s film work and the personalities behind it.

I created branded social templates, publishing plans, captions and hashtag approaches, then worked closely with the team to turn behind-the-scenes footage, bloopers, project stories and industry knowledge into regular content. The work covered planning as well as hands-on design, video editing and channel management.

The role also extended offline. I designed promotional materials for networking and industry events, including flyers, banners and exhibition graphics, and attended events such as the Childcare & Education Expo to capture photography and video for social coverage.
