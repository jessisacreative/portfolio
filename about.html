About

Marketing thinking with a hands-on creative practice.

I’m a Yorkshire-based creative working across marketing, communications, content, design and photography, primarily for museums, galleries and cultural organisations.

My work moves between strategy and making: developing campaign approaches, planning audiences and media, producing content, coordinating PR, designing assets and photographing exhibitions or events when a project needs it.

I’m particularly interested in work that helps cultural organisations feel relevant, accessible and worth people’s time.
