About Jess Watson

I sit between the campaign plan and the creative work people actually see.

I’m a Yorkshire-based marketing and creative professional working mainly with museums, galleries and cultural organisations.

My role changes with the project. Sometimes I’m building the campaign strategy and audience plan. Sometimes I’m writing the press release, setting up paid ads, shooting photographs, making reels, briefing designers or getting print into the real world. Usually, it is a mix.

That overlap is the point. I like understanding what a project needs to achieve and staying close enough to the making to help it get there.
