Jim Moir: Neo Fauna

Marketing, PR, content and campaign photography for Jim Moir's exhibition at Cartwright Hall Art Gallery, Bradford.

RoleCampaign lead · Marketing & PR
OrganisationBradford District Museums & Galleries
Exhibition28 March–31 August 2026

Campaign

Jess Watson led the marketing and PR campaign for Jim Moir: Neo Fauna at Cartwright Hall Art Gallery. The work brought together campaign planning, press activity, editorial content, photography and media-facing materials around the exhibition launch and run.

Campaign materials included a press release, an interview with Jim Moir, editorial/blog content, video footage and photography created for publicity and digital channels.

Photography

Jess also photographed Jim Moir at Cartwright Hall for the campaign. One of those photographs is used on Visit Bradford's public listing for Neo Fauna, where the image appears as “Jim Moir at Cartwright Hall Gallery.”

The campaign generated or supported coverage across regional and national outlets. Saved campaign coverage includes:
