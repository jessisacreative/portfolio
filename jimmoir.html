Jim Moir:
Neo Fauna

A campaign for Cartwright Hall that brought together exhibition marketing, press, social content and original photography.

Client

Venue

Role

Disciplines

Jim Moir: Neo Fauna was a useful example of the way I tend to work: the campaign, the content and the story were never really separate things.

I led marketing and communications around the exhibition as part of my work with Bradford Museums & Galleries, helping shape how it was presented to audiences across digital, social and press activity.

I also photographed Jim in the exhibition and documented the gallery itself. Those images became campaign assets in their own right, appearing across Bradford Museums channels and in external coverage. Rather than treating photography as an add-on, it became part of the communications toolkit from the beginning.

The project sits across several areas of this portfolio for that reason. This page tells the campaign story, while the strongest photographs and press outcomes can also be explored independently.
