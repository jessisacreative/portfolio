Content strategy / Social media

Social content
at Mediaworks

I joined Mediaworks as a Junior Content Producer, making social content for skincare and FMCG clients. TikTok was still new territory for many brands, so the job was part planning, part making: spotting formats worth trying, producing videos and photography, and helping the team get comfortable with the platform.

Agency
Mediaworks UK Ltd
Client sector
Global skincare and FMCG brands
Role
Junior Content Producer
Year
2021–2023

01 / Background & contribution

From scroll to shoot.

A lot of the thinking was practical: which format fits the idea, what makes someone stop scrolling, and what can we make well with the time and tools available? I moved between planning, filming, editing and reviewing what worked.

Some of the most useful work happened before the camera came out. I used platform trends and keyword research to shape ideas, then made short-form video, photography and social assets for TikTok and Instagram.

I also took the lead on TikTok content within the agency. I made training resources for colleagues and helped the team get to grips with the platform's formats, so TikTok could become part of the work we offered clients.

Alongside planned content, I appeared in videos and covered influencer events, turning what was happening on the day into material for social. It was quick-moving work, and I liked that ideas had to earn their place in the feed.

02 / Scope of work

The hands-on bit.
