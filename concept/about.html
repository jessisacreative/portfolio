03 / A bit about Jess

More than one thing.

I'm Jess — a multidisciplinary creative in West Yorkshire, with experience across arts and culture marketing, graphic design, photography, content and the ideas that connect them.

The person behind the projects

Hi, I'm Jess.

I currently work in a part-time job share as Marketing Manager at the National Science and Media Museum in Bradford, alongside freelance projects for arts and cultural organisations.

My work moves between strategic thinking and hands-on creative production: campaigns, design, photography, writing and creating the things that help people connect with ideas.

I studied Creative Advertising at Leeds Arts University, where I was shortlisted for Cannes Future Lions. I later returned to study Creative Practice, completing a PGCert.

Outside the day job, you'll probably find me photographing live music, exploring galleries, walking with my camera, or obsessing over a new creative experiment. I like the overlap between art, culture, technology and the slightly unexpected.
