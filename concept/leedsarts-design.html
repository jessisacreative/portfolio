Graphic Design

Leeds Arts University prospectus

Publication design and a new visual identity in print.

My role

Following the university’s website redesign, I helped carry the refreshed identity into print. The undergraduate prospectus was one of the largest pieces I redesigned.

From cover to interior

I applied the identity across the prospectus, including pages featuring alumni work. The cover and inside spread show how the same design works at two different scales.

Taking it into recruitment materials

I carried the design into bookmarks, postcards and event displays, prepared the artwork for print and worked with suppliers on production.
