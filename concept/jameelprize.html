01 / Background & contribution

Bringing the exhibition to Bradford audiences

Jameel Prize: Moving Images is the seventh edition of the V&A's prestigious triennial award for contemporary art and design inspired by Islamic culture, history, society and ideas. This edition focused on moving image and digital media, bringing together seven artists from across the Middle East and South Asia.

I was approached by Cartwright Hall's curator after a recommendation from Bradford 2025's head of communications. The brief: deliver a fast-turnaround marketing campaign with just 8 weeks until the exhibition closed. Late-stage marketing funds had been allocated, and I needed to maximise their impact across multiple channels.

This meant coordinating with design agencies, press teams, and print and distribution partners while managing stakeholder relationships across the V&A, Art Jameel, Bradford Council, and Bradford 2025. I secured local advertising across Leeds and Bradford: out-of-home, grassroots distribution, and press coverage including the Yorkshire Post.

For social media, I planned organic content and set up paid social ads, targeting various audience segments to reach both Bradford's diverse Muslim communities and broader audiences interested in contemporary art. Throughout the campaign, I actively monitored and optimised ad performance, making tweaks to maximise effectiveness within the limited timeframe. The campaign combined digital and out-of-home activity to connect the exhibition with audiences across Bradford and beyond.
