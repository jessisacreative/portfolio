# Jess Watson / @jessisacreative — Website redesign concept

Status: **working draft, not published**. The current public website has not been replaced.

## The idea

An editorial, multidisciplinary portfolio bringing together campaigns, design, original photography, published writing and creative experiments. It leads with Jess as a creative person while keeping professional case studies clear and useful.

Start at `concept/index.html`.

## Site structure

- Homepage: six selected projects, short introduction, photography, journal, playground and contact.
- Work: all ten projects, with category filters.
- Ten case-study pages: YOU:MATTER; Cartwright Hall 2026 Gallery Launches; Beyond the Frame; Sound + Vision; The Great Leeds Fungi Exhibition; University Branding & Marketing; Jameel Prize: Moving Images; The Art Market; Mediaworks social content; Reflections Film & Media.
- Photography: live music, exhibitions, event photography and original fine-art nature studies.
- Journal: one verified published article.
- About: experience and creative practice.
- Playground: a clearly labelled, non-interactive preview of the future 3D portfolio idea.

## Design and access

- Warm paper palette, expressive typography, dark editorial sections, lime and coral accents.
- Custom SVG monogram favicon applied consistently across all 15 HTML pages.
- Responsive layouts, descriptive image alt text, reduced-motion support and visible keyboard focus.
- Skip links move keyboard focus to the main content and account for the sticky header.
- Project filters use native buttons and update their pressed state.

## Content and asset notes

- The project descriptions and roles are based on Jess's career materials and existing portfolio content.
- The Cartwright Hall case study is framed around the verified freelance Campaign Coordinator role and the campaign for five new permanent and temporary galleries. Internal strategy themes and unverified campaign naming have been removed. Formal approvals and press-office distribution are attributed to Bradford Council and partner teams.
- Four original fine-art photographs are credited to Jess Watson, converted to WebP and kept under 700px on the longest edge.
- Campaign spend, ROAS and impression figures are not included.
- Third-party project images are inherited from the existing portfolio; portfolio-use rights still need confirmation where they are not already cleared.

## Static QA completed

All 15 HTML pages were checked for a single title and H1, a meta description, skip-link targets, image alt attributes, valid in-page anchors, and valid inline JavaScript. Structured data parses successfully; provisional site URLs are omitted until the production address is chosen. Focus-accent contrast is 4.63:1 on the paper background and 3.04:1 on the dark background.
