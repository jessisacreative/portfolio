02 / A little about me

Ideas first.
Labels later.

I'm Jess — a West Yorkshire-based creative with a background in advertising, design and cultural marketing.

By day, I'm a Marketing Manager at the National Science and Media Museum. Outside of work, I'm often photographing something, writing about music or getting absorbed in a new creative rabbit hole.

I like work that combines good ideas with thoughtful execution, whether that's a full-scale campaign, a piece of editorial design, or something that's just interesting to make.
