Cartwright Hall 2026 Gallery Launches

Campaign planning and marketing communications for the launch of five new permanent and temporary galleries at Cartwright Hall.

01 / Context & contribution

The story.

Cartwright Hall sits within Lister Park and has been part of Bradford's civic culture since 1904. After Bradford 2025, including the Turner Prize and Jameel Prize at the gallery, the 2026 programme welcomed five new permanent and temporary galleries.

I was retained by Bradford District Museums and Galleries to deliver the marketing and communications campaign for the launch of the five galleries. My work focused on campaign planning and coordinating visitor-facing communications around the launches.

The selected images document exhibition launches and installations. My work formed part of a wider partnership: formal approvals and press-office distribution were handled by Bradford Council and partner teams.
