01 / Background & contribution

A mix of planning and making.

Reflections makes film and educational content for early years settings. I was their freelance social media manager from 2022 to 2023, looking after their channels and giving the work a clear, consistent presence online.

That included planning posts, making and editing short videos, and creating promotional graphics for events. I also designed print and digital materials – from flyers to banners and shell-scheme graphics – for their presence at national industry events.

At industry events I photographed and filmed on site, then edited that material for the social channels.
