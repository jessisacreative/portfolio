Beyond the Frame

From a student identity to building-scale graphics.

My role

Katie Woodcock and Ben Pritchard-Swan created the selected identity. I took their artwork into print and onto the building, designing the exhibition guide, window vinyls and signage for Leeds Arts University.

Designing the exhibition guide

I put together the printed guide for the end-of-year show, which brought work from fifteen undergraduate courses into one exhibition.

Taking the design outside

I prepared the identity for print and designed the large window vinyls and signage. The images show how it appeared at the entrance and on poster towers around Leeds.
