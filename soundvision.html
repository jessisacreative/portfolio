Sound and Vision is the culmination of a multi-million pound transformation of the National Science and Media Museum. Two new permanent galleries spanning two floors showcase over 500 exhibits from the museum's collection of photography, film, television, animation, video games and sound technologies.

Working in a job share arrangement, I co-led the campaign with my partner, focusing on creative delivery and production. This meant turning the ambition of the galleries into campaign materials that could communicate the scale, diversity and local relevance of what we were launching.

I managed campaign planning and content strategy, working with design agencies and coordinating fast-turnaround photo and video production around installation and opening timelines.

I also managed print and out-of-home activity across Yorkshire, working directly with suppliers on poster towers, newspaper advertising and distribution, alongside content and launch-event activity.
