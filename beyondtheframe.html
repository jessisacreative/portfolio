Beyond the Frame

Leeds Arts University · 2024

Work → Beyond the Frame

Organisation
Leeds Arts University
Role
Marketing & Design Officer · Campaign Lead
Scope
Branding rollout, print, OOH, exhibition graphics
Year
2024

Beyond the Frame was Leeds Arts University's 2024 end-of-year exhibition, showcasing final-year work across fifteen undergraduate courses.

I managed the campaign from student briefing through to launch, including the selection process for the student-created identity and the rollout of the chosen visual system across digital and physical formats.

I prepared assets for campaign use, created promotional GIFs for a Leeds List partnership, organised poster-tower advertising, designed the printed exhibition guide, large-scale window vinyls and signage across the building.
