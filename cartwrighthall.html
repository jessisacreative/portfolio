Overview

One role, multiple exhibitions.

I worked across campaign planning and delivery for Bradford District Museums & Galleries, with a particular focus on Cartwright Hall. The work combined marketing and PR coordination with hands-on content production and photography.

Rather than treating the 2026 season as one generic campaign, the work adapted to distinct exhibitions, artists, partners and audiences while maintaining a coherent public-facing programme.
