Marketing & Communications

An exhibition campaign bringing together marketing, PR, photography and social content around Jim Moir’s new wildlife paintings at Cartwright Hall.

I worked across the campaign as part of my wider marketing and communications role for Bradford Museums & Galleries. The project included campaign planning, press activity, content production and photography around the exhibition install and launch.

The work is a useful example of how my roles overlap: the photographs were campaign assets in their own right, supported press and social coverage, and also form part of my photography portfolio independently of the campaign.
