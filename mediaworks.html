Mediaworks was my first major post-degree agency role and gave me a broad grounding across planning, social media and creative production.

I started in strategy and planning, working on brand and social audits, audience research, trend reporting, creative foundations and new-business thinking before moving further into social-first production.

I became a go-to person for TikTok, planning, filming and editing short-form content, creating internal training resources and helping establish platform best practice. I also worked across photography, design, influencer-event content, AR filters and television campaign support.
