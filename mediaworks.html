Mediaworks was my first major post-degree agency role and gave me an unusually broad grounding across planning, social media and creative production.

I started in strategy and planning, working on brand and social audits, audience research, trend reporting, creative foundations and new-business thinking. I used tools including YouGov Profiles, Ahrefs, Majestic and Meltwater to turn audience and market information into recommendations, content themes and campaign ideas.

As I moved into social and creative production, I became a go-to person for TikTok. I planned, filmed, edited and sometimes appeared in short-form content, created internal TikTok training resources and helped establish platform best practice inside the agency. I also produced photography and design assets for Instagram and worked at influencer events to capture social-first content.

The role also let me experiment with emerging formats. I created branded augmented-reality filters using Spark AR and Effect House, and contributed to wider creative work including television advertising, influencer PR and video production.
